### 🌍 Problem Statement
Large college hostel messes and cafeterias routinely overproduce food due to manual headcount guesswork. Meanwhile, surplus edible meals go to waste because coordinating with nearby NGOs takes too long before food spoils.

### 💡 How AnnapurnaAI Solves It
1. **📊 AI Kitchen Prep Advisor:** Predicts daily student meal attendance based on campus schedules (regular days, weekends, fests, and exam weeks) to prevent overcooking before service begins.
2. **📸 5-Second Vision AI Surplus Scanner:** Kitchen staff capture a photo of leftover trays on mobile or desktop. Vision AI identifies the dish, estimates meal portions, enforces mandatory hygiene checks, and assigns a freshness timer.
3. **🤝 Hyperlocal NGO Dispatch & OTP Handoff:** Broadcasts verified surplus batches to partner NGOs within a 5 km radius on an interactive Berhampur map and generates a 4-digit Pickup OTP for safe gate verification.
